This simulator implements a dynamic shadows system projecting light from the glass shapes onto a glowing physical gradient background.

Refraction & Shadow Physics:

  • Tactile Milky-White Tint: A soft white color is injected into the glass body (`transmission: 0.85`), making shapes clearly defined while retaining beautiful inner transparency.
  • Real Inter-Object Shadows: Shapes cast soft shadows onto each other and on the background, bringing realistic depth.
  • Exaggerated Edge Bevels: Extruded shapes features larger rounded bevel corners designed to catch specular highlight points and warp light heavily.
  • Index of Refraction: Fully adjustable up to 2.4 (the diamond threshold) to alter how dramatically light curves around the plate geometry.
